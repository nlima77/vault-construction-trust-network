# Private Reviewer Packet

## Subject

30 minutes of brutal feedback on a construction-records tool

## Message

Hi [name] — we’re building an early tool that organizes construction project documents — contracts, permits, invoices, photos — flags what’s missing, and exports a tamper-evident record packet a homeowner or insurer can independently verify.

It’s pre-launch, docs and a demo on fictional sample data only — no real customer documents, and nothing to buy or invest in.

We’re looking for blunt feedback from people who know construction, business operations, or legal/compliance. We’d rather hear “this is useless because X” now than after launch.

Attached: a 5-minute demo walkthrough and 10 questions. Anything you say stays private.

Can we get 30 minutes in the next two weeks?

## 10 reviewer questions

1. In your work, when did missing or unverifiable project paperwork last cost someone real money? What happened?
2. Watching the demo: what’s the first thing you don’t believe or don’t trust?
3. Who would pay for this — contractor, homeowner, insurer, nobody? How much, and at what moment?
4. What would you need to see before handing a packet like this to a client, buyer, or adjuster?
5. What existing tool or habit does this have to beat — email folders, Buildertrend, a shoebox?
6. Which claimed feature feels like overpromising?
7. What’s missing from the completeness checklist for a typical roof/remodel job in your region?
8. Does the tamper-evidence/hash layer matter to you at all, or is organization alone the value?
9. If you saw any crypto or financial-market association with this project, how would it change your opinion? Be honest.
10. Would you introduce us to one person who has this problem right now? Why or why not?

## Scoring

Score each 1–5 unless noted.

- Problem severity: this pain is real and expensive.
- Solution fit: demo addresses the pain.
- Willingness to use within 90 days.
- Willingness to pay: 0 = never, 5 = named a price.
- Credibility/trust in team and claims.
- Clarity: could re-explain accurately to a peer.
- Red-flag count: reverse-scored for hype/legal/privacy concerns.

Weight construction operators double on:

- Problem severity.
- Willingness to pay.

Go signal:

- Average problem severity ≥ 4.
- At least 3 reviewers offer an intro to someone with the problem.

Intros matter most because they are harder to fake with politeness.
