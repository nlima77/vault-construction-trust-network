# Governance — Advisory Circle

## Current recommendation

Do not create a formal legal board yet unless an attorney sets up the entity and governance structure.

Start with a small **Advisory Circle** of 3–5 trusted people.

## Advisory seats to recruit

1. **Construction operator / product use case** — practical contractor and homeowner workflow knowledge.
2. **Technical lead** — AI, web app, database, security, and product architecture.
3. **Blockchain/security reviewer** — proof layer, wallet safety, smart contract risk, privacy-preserving design.
4. **Legal/compliance advisor** — disclaimers, entity structure, securities/token risk, IP/license review.
5. **Industry customer voice** — realtor, insurance/restoration, GC, warranty/service, or property management perspective.

## Advisor boundaries

Advisors should help with feedback, risk review, product direction, and credibility.

Advisors should not be presented as controlling the token, treasury, original Vault AI project, or original social channels unless there is a formal legal structure and written authority.

## People to avoid

Avoid anyone whose first priority is token price, pump language, insider allocation, admin access, or hype.

## Public governance language

Safe language:

> We are forming a small advisory circle to review product direction, construction workflow, security, attribution, and compliance considerations.

Avoid language that implies a formal board, official original-team authority, token governance control, or securities-like promises.
