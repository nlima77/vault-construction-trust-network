# Governance — Advisory Circle

## Current recommendation

Do not create a formal legal board unless an attorney sets up the entity and governance structure.

Start with a small **Advisory Circle** of trusted reviewers.

## Advisory seats to recruit

1. **Construction operator / product use case** — practical contractor and homeowner workflow knowledge.
2. **Technical lead** — AI, web app, database, security, and product architecture.
3. **Security/privacy reviewer** — proof layer, data handling, access control, and privacy-preserving design.
4. **Legal/compliance advisor** — disclaimers, entity structure, IP/license review, and public-claims review.
5. **Industry customer voice** — realtor, insurance/restoration, GC, warranty/service, or property management perspective.

## Advisor boundaries

Advisors should help with feedback, risk review, product direction, and credibility.

Advisors should not be presented as a formal board, legal authority, investor group, or official original-project team unless there is a formal legal structure and written authority.

## Public governance language

Safe language:

> We are forming a small advisory circle to review product direction, construction workflow, security, attribution, and compliance considerations.

Avoid language that implies official original-team authority, securities-like promises, or legal conclusions.
