# Construction Vault — Private Reviewer Packet

## What this is

You're invited to review an early prototype of **Construction Vault** — an AI-powered document organization tool for construction contractors. This is a product demo, not a pitch. No investment opportunity. No token. No blockchain required.

## What to review

1. **Landing page** — Does the product make sense in 30 seconds?
   - URL: https://iridescent-mermaid-9bc487.netlify.app

2. **Interactive demo** — Does the workflow feel useful?
   - URL: https://iridescent-mermaid-9bc487.netlify.app/demo/index.html
   - Explore: project overview, document vault, metadata confirmation, checklist, timeline, proof manifest, export preview

3. **GitHub repo** — Is the project positioning clear and safe?
   - URL: https://github.com/nlima77/vault-construction-trust-network

## Reviewer profile we're looking for

- **Primary:** Contractors, GCs, remodelers, restoration firms, specialty trades who deal with project documentation
- **Secondary:** Insurance adjusters, real estate investors, anyone who touches construction records
- **Also useful:** People who can spot legal/financial language risks

## Review format (30 minutes max)

**Part 1 — First impression (5 min)**
- Open the landing page. What's your immediate reaction?
- Do you understand what it does without reading everything?

**Part 2 — Demo walkthrough (15 min)**
- Open the demo. Click through documents.
- Try the metadata confirmation toggles.
- Check the checklist and timeline.
- Would this save you time or headaches?

**Part 3 — Hard questions (10 min)**
- Would you actually use this? What would stop you?
- What feature is missing that would make it a no-brainer?
- Does anything feel risky, misleading, or overpromised?
- Would you pay for this? If so, what price feels fair?
