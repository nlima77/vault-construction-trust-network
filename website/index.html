Questions

Is this a real product or just a demo?
It's a working prototype built on real construction workflows. The demo uses 100% synthetic data. Private beta access is real — we're testing with select contractors in Texas before general availability.
Do I need to understand blockchain or crypto to use this?
No. The proof layer uses SHA-256 hashes — standard cryptography that's been around for decades. No wallet, no tokens, no blockchain knowledge required. The MVP works entirely offline if needed.
What happens to my documents?
You keep full ownership. Documents are processed in isolated environments. We don't train AI models on your data, sell analytics, or share records with third parties. Privacy is the default, not an upsell.
Can this replace my existing document storage?
Eventually, yes. Today it's designed to sit alongside whatever you use — Dropbox, Google Drive, local folders — and add structure, classification, and proof metadata on top. Export packets can live anywhere.
Is this connected to the original Vault AI project?
This is a community-led effort inspired by the original Vault AI open-source project (MIT licensed, by pashpashpash). We are unaffiliated with the original team unless formal written authorization is established later. We maintain attribution and respect the original work.
Who's building this?
Neil Lima, president of Certipro Home Improvement LLC — a real outdoor living contractor in the Dallas-Fort Worth area. He's building the tool he wishes he had for his own projects.
