Construction Record Vault

Organized, portable, tamper-evident construction project documentation.

Own your records. Verify your project packet.

The problem

Construction records scatter across email, phones, portals, folders, and text threads. Years later, owners, contractors, adjusters, inspectors, and buyers need a clean project history they can rely on.

The product direction

The first MVP is a Project Record Packet: synthetic project records organized by type, timeline, completeness checklist, and privacy-preserving proof metadata.
